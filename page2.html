Gordy Dining Hall

Gordy dining hall has as variety of all you can eat options this includes:

  • Homestyle favorites at main plate
  • Freshly made pizza at Villa Toscana
  • Freshly made sandwhiches at a deli station
  • Options for vegan and vegetarians

The nook
